## The concept in one line

The family lota, remembered — a grandmother's shelf, a mother's bathroom, now a founder's backpack: the same object, three generations, pocket-sized. Told as a first-person heritage piece with warmth and zero lecture.

## Casting routes (same two as angle 2 — decide at shoot time)

- **(a) Arsal on camera (preferred for this angle):** talking-head beats to camera + hands-only cut-aways. His story, his face — founder-native trust, and the direct counter to the AI-callout.
- **(b) Arsal hands-only + VO:** warm first-person narration over object shots; no face needed.

## Format

- **Length:** 30s organic hero cut. A 15s boost cut is mapped below but stays bench-warmed unless Arsal greenlights spend.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical, native phone camera, 1080p60 min / 4K60 preferred (60fps keeps the faucet readable at half-speed ramps).
- **Look:** handheld, natural home light — kitchen window, warm bulb, no studio anything. No LUTs, contrast-only grade; micro-shake stays (the realism credential).
- **Audio:** diegetic (chai cup, pressure-cooker hiss, faucet, plastic-bottle squeeze) + one soft trending audio bed at edit. VO below optional.
- **Language:** English VO with two-to-three Urdu words kept natural (aapa, nani, "lota hi lota hai") — code-switching is the authenticity signal for this audience. Subtitle everything for sound-off.
