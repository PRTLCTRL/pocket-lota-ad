## The concept in one line

Travel-day comfort told from a woman's toiletry-kit POV: the long flight/road trip, the hotel bathroom, and the one thing in the kit that makes her feel *set* — no lecture, no medical talk, just "I don't travel without it."

**Tone:** calm, warm, aspirational-mundane. The opposite of angle 1's agitation. No scare copy, no "as you age" talk — the Sep 15 research says this segment converts on *dignity and discretion*, not fear.

## Format

- **Length:** 15s hero cut (if ever boosted) + 30s organic cut
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical, native phone camera, 1080p60 min / 4K60 if available
- **Look:** handheld, natural light — hotel bathroom warm-white, kit flat-lay by a window. Light warp stabilization MAX; micro-shake stays. No LUTs, contrast-only grade.
- **Audio:** diegetic (zipper, unwrap of hotel paper, tap on the counter) + soft acoustic/lo-fi trending audio at edit. VO optional — text-on-screen carries it if no voice.
- **The performer:** hands-only or face-of-a-45+-woman. If face: minimal, smiling, talking-head beats only, no reenactment.
