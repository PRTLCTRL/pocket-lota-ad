## The concept in one line

A workday told in bathroom trips — the day gets more stressful, the bathroom gets worse, the fix is already in the bag. Founder-POV, vertical 9:16, shot entirely on a phone.

## Format

- **Length:** 15s hero cut (boost unit) + 30s organic cut. Trim points marked per shot.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical, native phone camera (4K60 if available, 1080p60 minimum — 60fps keeps water readable at half-speed ramps).
- **Look:** handheld, real office/commute light. Do NOT stabilize in post beyond a light warp — micro-shake is the realism credential. No color grade beyond +contrast; no LUTs.
- **Audio:** diegetic sound (door latch, faucet, HVAC hum) + trending low-percussion audio laid at edit. Voiceover optional, written below.
