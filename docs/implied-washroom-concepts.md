## 1. The director techniques (how the viewer KNOWS she's seated in a washroom without seeing it)

The tell stack: **geometry + environment texture + sound + ritual objects** —
each technique picks 2–3 of these, never the fixture itself.

| # | Technique | The frame shows | What reads "seated in the washroom" | Avoids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **The tilt-down POV** | Her POV looking down: hand + bottle (black cap off) + tight floor tiles, pour arc exits frame bottom | Camera geometry says "seated"; tile texture + reverb say washroom | Bowl, stall, body |
| T2 | **The closed-door concert** | Camera OUTSIDE the stall door (the OCCUPIED slider flips red), hold 3s, muffled pour + one soft exhale SFX, then door opens to her exit | Sound does 100% of the work; the door-latch is the ritual | Everything — nothing is shown at all |
| T3 | **The mirror crop** | Bathroom-mirror reflection, tight: hand + bottle + arc only, blurred tile background behind | Mirrors = washroom, universally; the crop hides everything else | Fixture reflections, her face (optional) |
| T4 | **The knee-frame** | Shot from hip height: her hand holds the bottle below-frame, trouser fabric + knee edge visible at frame bottom | The fabric + low camera = seated read; pour arc off-frame | Any fixture, any position detail |
| T5 | **The floor-POV** | Camera low on tile floor near her flats/pumps; the bottle enters from above, arc pours down off-frame | Tiles + shoes + reverb; the scale says bathroom floor | Anything above knee height |
| T6 | **The shoe-shuffle** | Only her shoes visible under the stall door gap… **NO — VIOLATES the stall-gap lock (office list shot 5 was cut-away only).** Listed to show it was considered and rejected | — | — |

**Recommended core: T1 (tilt-down) for the pour money-shot + T2 (closed-door) as the setup/exit frame.** They compose into one continuous beat: door sign → 3s closed door with pour SFX → cut INSIDE to the tilt-down pour for the final 1.5s → exit + hand-wash outro. The viewer's imagination does the sitting.

## 2. Audio architecture (the "sound does the work" rule, proven since the office shot list)

- Tile-bathroom reverb on everything inside (HVAC hum, distant hand-dryer)
- Door latch + slide of the OCCUPIED indicator
- **The pour: 2–3 seconds, muffled through the door (T2) then crisp in the tile-reverb POV (T1)** — the product SFX moment
- One soft exhale (relief, dignity — never strain/comedy)
- Heels click-past in the corridor (the office-world reminder)
- Sources: Kling v3 native audio (generate_audio) or fal's
  `elevenlabs/sound-effects/v2` for specific one-shots (latch, pour).
